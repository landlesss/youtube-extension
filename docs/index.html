01

Overview

YouTube Subtitle Downloader ("the extension", "we") lets you download, copy, and translate subtitles from YouTube videos. Subtitle extraction happens locally in your browser. Signing in and translation involve our backend service at downloadsubtitles.online ("the service"). This policy covers both.

02

What we collect

Only what's needed to run the features you actually use:

Account
Your Google email address and Google account ID, obtained when you sign in with Google.
Subtitle text
The caption lines of a video, sent to our service only when you use the Translate feature.
Video ID
The YouTube video's ID, sent when you download, copy, or translate — used to enforce the daily free-plan limit per video.
Usage
Download/translation counts and character usage tied to your account, for plan limits and billing.
Local prefs
Your session token and a one-time "rate us" flag, stored only in your browser via chrome.storage.local.

Downloading or copying subtitles without translation does not send the subtitle text itself to our service — it's extracted and written to a file entirely on your device. Signing in is still required so the daily video limit can be enforced.

03

What we don't collect

  • No browsing history beyond the specific video you act on
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad network, no third-party analytics/tracking pixels
  • No payment card details (handled entirely by our payment processor if you upgrade — we never see or store card numbers)
  • We never sell or rent your data to anyone
04

How we use it

  • To authenticate you and keep your session signed in
  • To enforce the free-plan daily limit and your character quota
  • To generate translations via our translation provider
  • To diagnose and fix bugs (e.g. failed translation requests)
05

Who we share it with

A short, fixed list — no data brokers, no ad exchanges:

  • Google — to verify your identity when you sign in
  • Our translation provider — receives the subtitle text you ask to translate, solely to return a translation
  • Our hosting/infrastructure providers — run the servers our service operates on, under standard confidentiality terms

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the security of the service against abuse.

If you rate the extension 4-5 stars, we open the Chrome Web Store review page. If you rate it 1-3 stars, we open a Google Form instead, so we can follow up on feedback privately rather than it becoming a public review. Either way this is a link you choose to click — we don't submit anything on your behalf. If you fill out the feedback form, we can see what you submit there; the form itself is hosted and operated by Google under Google's own privacy policy.
06

Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account (see Contact), we delete your account record and stop retaining new usage data going forward. Subtitle text sent for translation is processed to return a result and is not kept longer than necessary to do that and to prevent abuse of the service.

07

Security

All traffic between the extension and our service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to account data is limited to what's needed to operate the service. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security — but we don't treat that as an excuse not to take reasonable precautions.

08

Your choices

  • Sign out any time from the extension panel — this clears your local session
  • Revoke access from your Google Account permissions page at any time
  • Delete your account by contacting us (see below) — we'll remove your account record
  • Uninstall the extension at any time from chrome://extensions, which also clears all local data it stored
09

Children

This extension is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

10

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "last updated" date below and, where appropriate, note it in the extension's release notes.
