How it works

01

Open a video

Go to any YouTube video that has captions — auto-generated ones work too.

02

Tap the icon

A small round button appears on the page. Click it to open the subtitle panel.

03

Pick language & format

Choose the caption track and SRT, VTT, or TXT — the preview updates instantly.

04

Download, copy, or translate

Get the original text, or translate it into another language first — either replaces the original or sits under it, line by line.

How your Google account is used

To download, copy, or translate subtitles, the extension asks you to sign in with Google. That is the only thing Google sign-in is used for.

What we ask Google for

  • Your email address
  • Your Google account ID
  • Your basic profile (openid, email, profile scopes)

Why we need it

To recognize your account and apply the free plan's daily limit. We never read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or any other Google data, and we don't access your YouTube account — captions are read from the video page you have open.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
